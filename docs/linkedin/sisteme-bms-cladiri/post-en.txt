A non-residential building in Romania with more than 290 kW of heating or air conditioning? The BACS obligation in Law 372/2005 has applied since 31 December 2024.

The threshold is per system family (heating, air conditioning, or combined with ventilation), and the obligation applies where technically and economically feasible. The 70 kW threshold in Directive (EU) 2024/1275 comes next, but is not yet transposed into Romanian law.
